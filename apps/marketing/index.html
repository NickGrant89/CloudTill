The product

Three surfaces. One PurePOS.

PurePOS is the till, the back office, and the ordering system — not a WordPress bolt-on, not a Loyverse wrapper. Pick Essential, Complete, or Multi; items and reports live in the cloud and browser apps stay in sync.

  • 1

    Cloud till

    Browser / tablet register with offline queue — keep selling when wifi drops, sync when you’re back.

  • 2

    Back office

    Menu, customers, stock, staff, billing, and reports — managed once for every till.

  • 3

    Online ordering

    Customers order into the same store; kitchen sees the tickets on Complete.

Offline is built into the till

Open the register once online so the menu caches. If the network drops, the till shows Offline / Sync and queued sales push to the cloud when you reconnect.
