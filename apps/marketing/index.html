The product

Three surfaces. One cloud.

Catalog and reports live in the cloud. Tills and ordering are browser apps that stay in sync.

    Cloud till

    Browser / tablet register with offline queue — keep selling when wifi drops, sync when you’re back.

  • 3

    Online ordering

    Customers order into the same store; kitchen sees the tickets on Pro.

Offline is built into the till

Load the register once online, then keep selling if the network drops. The till shows Offline / Sync; queued sales push to the cloud when you reconnect.
