Cloud till

Built for the rush — even when Wi‑Fi isn’t

Open the register once online so the menu caches. If the network drops, sales queue locally and push up when you reconnect.

  • Fast item grid & barcodes Categories, SKU / barcode lookup, and favourites / quick keys for your busiest lines.
  • Open tickets & hold / recall Park a tab, reopen it on another till, and keep the floor moving with a live tickets board.
  • Payments that match the counter Cash, card, gift card, split tender, discounts, VAT, and tip presets with round-up.
  • Receipts & drawer HTML and ESC/POS printing, drawer kick, voids, refunds, and reprints from the same sale.
  • Shifts & offline sync Cash-up shifts, Sync badge for queued sales, and export if you need a local backup before clearing browser data.

Back office

Run the shop once — every till stays in sync

Catalogue and people live in the cloud. Change an item, price, or stock level and the register picks it up on refresh.

  • Items, modifiers & kits Groups, variants, modifiers, and combo kits with branch assignment when you grow past one site.
  • Stock, transfers & stocktake Inventory counts, low-stock glances, multi-store transfers on Group, and barcode label helpers.
  • Customers & gift cards Loyalty, store credit, and gift cards that work on the till — import customers by CSV when you migrate.
  • Staff, PINs & time clock Owner, manager, and cashier roles with PIN login and a simple time clock for the floor.
  • Reports, EOD & backup Sales views, end-of-day CSV export, kitchen printer settings, and store backup / restore from Settings.

Ordering that lands on the kitchen board

Web ordering for pickup, dine-in, or delivery uses the same catalogue. From Shop upward, Just Eat, Deliveroo, Uber Eats and more feed the same kitchen queue the till already uses.
